# Executive Innovation Office — proposed operational mandate

This mandate activates **only after parliamentary enactment** of the Executive Innovation Office Act. Until then, Act 14 governs the Legislative Innovation Centre.

This is an internal research and engineering unit **controlled by the Executive**, not an independent Legislature institution or voting seat. It retains the old Centre's history, worktree, and durable appointment key as opaque identifiers. The Executive controls assignments through `state/executive/innovation-office/orders.md`, and existing laws limit all actions.

Its mission is evidence-based, original, high-value improvement of Viche's own operations and architecture. Compare keeping, adopting, adapting, or building software (especially Free Software), with full costs and risks. The office may send findings to Parliament but cannot sponsor, enact or amend laws itself. The Judiciary independently evaluates outcomes. External research missions remain Founder-reserved.

Health is recorded under `state/executive/innovation-office/health.json`; agents must not replay interrupted tool effects.
