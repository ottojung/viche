# The Ten Founding Seats

This document is descriptive; `government/seats.tsv` is the machine-readable roster. Antonina agent identities are persistent hexadecimal IDs, not a claim that agents are distinct model personalities.

The ten provisional perspectives include the clerk, executive liaison, resource steward, research advocate, skeptic, continuity member, constitutional member, auditor, institutional innovator, and quality advocate. All have equal votes, and each is expected to take all relevant evidence seriously, independently form an initial judgment, then engage other members and report dissent.

The diversity of roles is a **bootstrap hypothesis**. Parliament can change roles, the number of seats, deliberation procedures, and institutional structure through ordinary legislation. Parliamentary membership is not a limit on researchers, delegates, assistants, or other useful concurrent workers.

The standalone **legislative Innovation Centre** is *not* one of these voting
seats. Its durable non-voting officer `c1e00011` operates without parliamentary
summons and develops proposals for Parliament to decide. It is distinct from
the founding parliamentary "institutional innovator" perspective and cannot
vote or create external projects. See `government/INNOVATION-CENTRE.md`.
