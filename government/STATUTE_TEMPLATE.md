# Suggested statutory template

This template is drafting guidance, not a constitutional requirement. Parliament should improve it as it learns.

- **Title and motivating observation:** What is the observed problem, opportunity, or Founder preference? What evidence supports it?
- **Purpose:** What kind of improvement should this law accomplish, and how might it make the Founder happier?
- **Responsible authority:** Which institution must implement it? What may be delegated without another vote?
- **Operative text:** What is permitted, required, or prohibited? Distinguish a durable general rule from a one-time executive mandate.
- **Evidence and success:** What outputs and observations would distinguish implementation from merely claiming success? What would falsify the hypothesis?
- **Review or expiry:** When and under what conditions should it be reconsidered?
- **Parliamentary history:** Sponsor, debate/hearing, exact votes, dissent, adoption date, enactment record, Git commit.

## Example — System Performance Investigation Act
The Research Office shall investigate reports of excessive RAM use, beginning with existing Antonina host-daemon telemetry. The executive may commission any useful number of independent research fronts; it should avoid duplicate or mutually interfering work. A measured report with alternatives and a reproducible remediation test is due for parliamentary review. No worker-count quota is introduced.

## Example — Continuity Rehearsal Act
Before the externally enforced restart after 24 hours of box uptime, the executive records outstanding mandates and active work. After restart, the clerk reconciles interruptions and commissions continuation without duplicating live work. The auditor shall report recovered, lost and accidentally repeated work over three restart cycles.

## Example — Five-Day Public Account Act
The clerk shall propose and test a way for the Founder to see five days of decisions, votes, tasks, failures and results in roughly ten minutes, possibly as a website. The mechanism is left open for institutional invention. An initial version must be assessed against an unstructured board feed.
