# Operational refactor: responsibility instead of scripted control

Founder requested a deletion-oriented redesign of Viche after recurring failures
of scripted recovery and misleading process-health summaries.

This change retains the Founder-controlled constitutional bootstrap, ordinary
law, process identity, durable appointments, protected worktrees and S6 service
separation. It does **not** enact the pending Executive Innovation Office Act.
It changes only the ordinary operational dispatch architecture.

The ordinary government bootstrap launches each independent office through a
single availability-only agentic runner. The constitutional sitting invokes a
short role mandate instead of a scripted incident catalogue. Decisions about
what to investigate, repair, deliver and verify belong to agents and their
independent institutional reviewers. Responsibility journals are in durable
state outside Git and survive appointments.

The old watchdogs, metric collectors, audit-cycle prompt builders, liveness
classifiers and specialized office cycles must not remain on the automatic
dispatch path. They can be retired separately as their existing legal
procedures are reviewed; diagnostic utilities remain available for officers.
The Recovery Steward remains a separate, independently supervised agent and
must reconcile uncertain external effects before replacements act.

Unresolved continuity cases from before this cutover are **not closed**. Their
evidence and history remain in viche-state, and the Judiciary must independently
verify recovery. No process-state metric alone claims that the refactor worked.

Operational success criteria: independently functioning offices, preserved
separation of powers, no duplicate writes under failed continuations, and
observable progress on real obligations, demonstrated by artifacts rather than
an agent count or a green script.
