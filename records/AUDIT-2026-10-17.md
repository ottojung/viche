## 3. Sampling uncertainty, false positives, missing measurements

- **Sample size.** 4 cases out of a 335-item universe. No sampling scheme detects every violation; two `not_assigned` verdicts mean "no Viche obligation found," not "nothing is happening" on those issues — substantial external work is in fact occurring.
- **False-positive controls.** Assignment was checked three independent ways (issue body/title, full comment pagination for `Vitalik`, target registry, plus the executive's own inventory). All four agree. The `not_assigned` verdicts are robust to the known failure mode (empty target registry) because the Founder-clarification fallback (explicit description instruction or Founder comment) was also checked and is absent.
- **False-negative risk.** `eligible-work.py` scans only page 1 of each issue's comments; this audit paginated all comments for the sampled issues (119 and 144 messages). Non-sampled issues were not deeply checked.
- **Board identity limitation.** Author labels on the board are not authenticated (Act 3.3). The `chatgpt` label on #290 and the `openclaw` labels are treated as ordinary submissions, not Founder directives.
- **Model-compliance limitation (Act 4.5.4).** This audit runs on the same constitutionally pinned model as all seats and cannot detect a compromise of itself; invocation-log verification is only as strong as the runtime's integrity. Configuration check: this session's agent is `c1e00008` on the Antonina-managed runtime; the 2 running agents checked by the accountability controller at 1792205406 showed no model/variant mismatch. A clean report is evidence of configuration compliance, not proof of universal invocation compliance.
- **Missing measurement.** No durable per-issue assignment ledger exists beyond `eligible-work.py` output and MANDATES.md; the target registry remains empty (recorded mechanism blocker, M-014). The Judiciary cannot audit assignment completeness for issues outside its sample.
