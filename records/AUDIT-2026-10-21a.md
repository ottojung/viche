- **Recurring probe artifact.** Three `telemetry_unavailable` incidents (`1792529151`,
  `1792536588`, `1792546733`) appeared in a ~4-hour window, each a single failed agent-status probe
  during an executive restart, each cleared on re-run. The watchdog's `agent_status_ok` is a
  point-in-time probe of one resolved executive id; a restart transition reads as an incident for one
  interval even though the office is healthy. The signal is an "investigate" trigger, not proof of a
  sustained outage or of misconduct. The dispatch cadence then re-dispatches the same incident,
  producing repeat audits for a non-persistent condition.
