## Limitations and uncertainty

This is a bounded randomized sample, not a census. Two cases are unassigned research items, so the sample says little about assigned-work neglect or false delivery rates. The external threads show observable scope/design/reporting gaps; the final `not_assigned` verdicts mean those gaps were not established as Viche violations, not that those external issues are complete. The government decision identifier is malformed for board lookup, limiting that case to artifact verification. Outcomes are auditor assertions, not machine-verified proofs. Same-model dependence and unknown instrument error rates remain material limitations. A “verified” or “not_assigned” result is not a certificate of overall performance.

The Judiciary did not propose, sponsor, ratify, or implement reforms. The Legislature must consider the evidence under Act 15 §5. The Executive may correct board trails under existing law. This draw does not measure the Founder’s direct happiness and cannot certify whether Viche is making the Founder happier overall.
