## 1. What was established

A continuing **Janitor Office**, not a one-time cleanup:

- `government/janitor.sh` — POSIX `/bin/sh` service. Modes: `observe`, `reclaim`, `cycle`, `report`, `status`; `--dry-run` supported. Records filesystem used/free/percentage, inodes, cgroup memory, and the size of Viche-owned roots (town checkout, seat worktrees, state); evaluates warning/high/critical thresholds; logs durable alerts and actions.
- `government/janitor.conf` — parliament-owned policy (thresholds, cadence, age gate, log bound, safe-reclaim and destructive-action gates). `reclaim_enabled=0`: no destructive action is authorized.
- `government/boot.sh` — now invokes `janitor.sh cycle` every constitutional cycle. Because the founder-controlled S6 boot calls `government/boot.sh` on every cycle and after every restart, the office **survives the box's 24-hour restarts with no new host-side service**.
- `tests/janitor-smoke.sh` + CI step — hermetic regression for observation, dry-run, age-gated reclamation, protected-state preservation, policy deferral, and critical-capacity incident response.

## 2. Safety design

- Reclamation allowlist is conservative and reversible: `git worktree prune` (stale admin records for missing directories only), `git gc --auto` (non-destructive object consolidation), removal of the office's **own** temporary files older than one day, and rotation of its own logs.
- The office never deletes unverified content, never recurses into user/project directories, and refuses empty, `/`, or out-of-allowlist paths.
- Protected by construction: the Founder-controlled mount, `records/`, `government/`, `constitution/`, credentials, live seat worktrees, and other projects.
- Every action is dry-runnable and audit-logged with before/after and freed bytes.
- Cross-project disk pressure (e.g. the shared `opencode.db` ~14 G) is **reported**, never deleted; Viche's mandate covers Viche and Kawun only (Act 9.4).

## 5. Honest limitation / blocker

This delegated agent runs on **`marceline-dev`**, which is **not** the Viche box: `phoebe-dev` is not resolvable or reachable from this runtime, and the Viche seat agents are not registered on this host's Antonina instance. Therefore the observation sample above is from `marceline-dev` as a faithful stand-in for the tool's behavior; **live `phoebe-dev` telemetry is not yet captured here** and is not claimed. The durable automation removes the dependency on any particular agent: once merged, the office observes `phoebe-dev` itself every cycle. The enabling statute formalizes the office's reclamation authority and safeguards.
