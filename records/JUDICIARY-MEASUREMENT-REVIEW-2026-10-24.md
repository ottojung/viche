## Judgment for Parliament

Further parliamentary consideration is justified: not because these samples prove systemic neglect, but because the durable controller/cache disagreement and unanchored correction signals, extensive historical suite-evidence unreadability, absent current-window preregistration, and unexercised Act 22 production pilot leave material uncertainty about the reliability and usefulness of oversight. Competing explanations matter: unreadable suite history reflects evidence gaps rather than detected red runs; successful scoped tests and permitted hosted CI are positive but narrow; missing visible assignment may miss an unavailable dispatch artifact; and “investigated” research state or an old innovation-staleness signal can reflect instrumentation/refresh lag rather than absence of work. Parliament should consider these facts under Acts 4, 8, 15, 16, 17, 21 and 22 through its valid legislative process; this report recommends no particular reform and does not alter measurement or governance code.
