# Act 23 section 4.1: mandatory operational duty mapping

This mapping is a proposed cutover gate, NOT evidence that any old case is
resolved. Each entry must be independently checked against original law.

| Retired mechanism and statutory duty | Accountable owner | Durable evidence required | Independent verifier | Pass/fail criterion and gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Act 15: audit-cycle.sh randomized investigation | Judiciary | audit-selection.py selection provenance and Git-anchored records/AUDIT evidence | Clerk and non-reviewed parliamentary member | Bounded independent sampled review in each legal window; unsupported green status fails |
| Act 15: branch-liveness.sh and branch-health.sh | Executive continuity; Judiciary inquiry | original Antonina invocation status, logs, independent oversight-watch.sh evidence, office journal | Judiciary | Every interruption remains visible and recoverable until independent review |
| Act 17: institutional-cases.py corrective case generation and verification | Executive repair; Judiciary verification | original viche-state/institutional-cases/index.json and case JSON, append-only correction trail | Judiciary separate from repairer | No case closure absent independently checked original terminal effects |
| Act 17: correction-integrity audit | Judiciary | records/AUDIT and audit-accountability.py outputs | Clerk or other independent parliamentary member | No prior correction described as operative without legal evidence |
| Act 18: outcome-metrics.py and scripted board delivery summary | Clerk reporting, Executive substantiation | board-trail-refresh.sh generated complete index, board records, Town Hall 302 messages, verified work artifacts | Judiciary | Correct sourced changes in statutory reporting window, not commit or agent-count proxies |
| Act 19: branch-bootstrap.sh, office-specific cycles, seat continuity | Clerk for seats; Executive for officers; Recovery Steward for incapacitated agents | records/handoffs, handoff-queue.sh, historical appointment and terminal logs, office-blockers receipts | Judiciary | Terminal or running writers cannot replay, blockers are Clerk-visible; successor verifies external effects |
| Act 19: office-continuity.sh scripted reset | Recovery Steward | terminal invocation evidence, replacement appointment, continuity journal | Judiciary | Open obligations survive arbitrary failure, no duplicate side effect, independent verification |
| Act 21: exact-commit quality gate | Clerk | exact commit SHA/tree, clean isolated worktree tests/run-all.sh, independent review and CI | Judiciary for statutory review | No merge/deploy on absent exact-commit test, reviewer, clean status, or verified evidence |
| Act 23: old case inventory and closure | Executive repairs, Judiciary verifies | records/agentic-cutover-open-cases.md and original viche-state case JSON | Judiciary | Named owner and evidence for every open case, no silent closure on process exit |

Mechanical identity, permission, physical-resource, audit integrity, tally,
board index and oversight tools remain when required. Agent reasoning owns
open-ended decisions, not shell-based process success heuristics.
