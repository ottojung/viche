# Assumptions Registry

*Durable assumptions schedule per enacted law (Act 16 §2). Each entry records
load-bearing claims, the observable evidence that would falsify each, and a
review date. This is the standing input to the Judiciary's review and to
Act 10/13 sunset logic.*

## Maintenance

- New enacted laws MUST have an assumptions schedule before or at enactment.
- The Judiciary reviews this registry during each audit cycle.
- Assumptions that are falsified MUST be recorded with evidence and the law
  reconsidered at the next sitting.
