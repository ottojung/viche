This is an ordinary-law amendment to Act 34 §§2–3. It carries forward the preserved safeguards of Act 34 §4, §7 and Act 32/33 where those remain operative; it departs only by removing the special pre-start procedural machinery named in §3. It does not create paid-spending authority or weaken any substantive control.
