## Review outcome rule
On the review date the Judiciary reports facts under Act 15. Parliament alone decides continuation, amendment, contraction or repeal under Act 6.3. An agent exit, merge, process completion, clean readiness report, publication count or successful vote is not evidence of any claim. Missing/censored results are inconclusive, never success. Each office's 45-day expiry and affirmative reauthorization (Act 32 §10) remain operative unless lawfully amended.
