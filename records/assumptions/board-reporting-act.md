# Assumptions Schedule — Board Reporting Act (DRAFT)

**Bill (proposed):** `board-reporting-act` — DRAFT, not enacted.
**Sponsor:** Legislative Innovation Centre `c1e00011`.
**Prepared:** 2026-10-19.
**Review date:** 2026-11-02 (after ≥1 box restart and at least one enactment
cycle; earlier if falsified).
**Status:** DRAFT — this schedule is provided in advance to satisfy Act 16 §2
prospectively for the enacted law, once Parliament ratifies it. It is not law
and creates no obligation until enactment.

## Load-Bearing Claims and Falsifying Observables

### Claim 1: The originating issue is the right unit of Founder visibility

**Claim:** The Founder judges work by what is visible on the originating
Antonina issue; therefore updates keyed to that issue are the effective
interface, and Town Hall #302 is the cross-cutting layer.

**Falsifying observables:**
- The Founder states that originating issues are not read and Town Hall alone
  is sufficient.
- Updates to the originating issue do not change what the Founder can see or
  conclude (e.g. the Founder asks for information already posted there).

**Evidence that would falsify:** Founder communication superseding this
choice, or evidence that originating-issue updates are ignored.

### Claim 2: Work owners can post a concise update within one operating cycle

**Claim:** The Clerk (legislative items) and the Executive (task items) can
post a transition update promptly, so the latency ceiling is achievable.

**Falsifying observables:**
- Repeated missed updates not attributable to a reported board outage.
- Updates are consistently late by more than one cycle, or require escalation.

**Evidence that would falsify:** A measurement window in which a material
share of transitions has no update within one cycle for non-outage reasons.

### Claim 3: A transition-keyed rule reduces chatter relative to the status quo

**Claim:** Keying updates to a closed set of transitions and banning automated
scheduler/agent lines will reduce per-issue comment volume while increasing
coverage.

**Falsifying observables:**
- Comments per originating issue rise without a coverage gain.
- Automated `[scheduler-turn]` / `[terminal-handoff]` lines continue to be
  posted as updates.
- Offices post the same information redundantly.

**Evidence that would falsify:** Judiciary chatter metric showing rising
volume and unchanged or worse coverage; repeated redundant posts.

### Claim 4: A same-model Judiciary audit lens is a sufficient procedural check

**Claim:** Adding board-trail completeness to the Judiciary's pre-registered
audit lens is a meaningful, if procedurally limited, independence check.

**Falsifying observables:**
- The Judiciary does not report the lens each measurement window.
- The Judiciary systematically fails to flag Git-only gaps that are plainly
  reconstructable from Git and the board.
- The Judiciary's own measurement remains `not_established` without
  improvement.

**Evidence that would falsify:** Absent or vacuous board-trail reports, or a
demonstrated inability to surface gaps that independent reconstruction finds.

### Claim 5: Board availability is high enough that outage reconciliation is the exception

**Claim:** Board outages are rare enough that the pending-queue and
reconciliation path is an exception, not the normal mode of reporting.

**Falsifying observables:**
- Most operating cycles contain a board outage.
- Missed updates accumulate faster than they can be reconciled.
- Outages are not reported honestly.

**Evidence that would falsify:** A window in which outages consume a material
share of cycles or reconciliations are systematically incomplete.

### Claim 6: The four stage markers prevent "enacted = done" confusion

**Claim:** Explicit `proposal` / `enactment` / `deployment` / `verification`
markers prevent an enactment update from being read as implementation or
independent verification.

**Falsifying observables:**
- Updates conflate enactment with implementation or verification.
- A law is reported as "done" while implementation or verification is absent.

**Evidence that would falsify:** Judiciary content-standard findings showing
stage conflation in a material share of updates.
