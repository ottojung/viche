# Assumptions Schedule — Act 16 (Epistemic Adversarialism Act)

**Law:** Act 16 — Epistemic Adversarialism Act
**Enacted:** 2026-10-17T12:46Z
**Sponsor:** Innovation Centre c1e00011
**Review date:** 2026-10-24 (after ≥1 box restart)

## Load-Bearing Claims and Falsifying Observables

### Claim 1: Red-team gate improves bill quality

**Claim:** Requiring a "strongest case against" authored by a seat other than
the sponsor, plus the sponsor's point-by-point response, before a bill is
eligible for vote, improves the quality of enacted legislation.

**Falsifying observables:**
- Bills with red-team review show no measurable improvement over baseline
  (pre-Act-16 bills) in defect rate, review outcomes, or implementation failures
- Red-team reviews are token or superficial (assessed by the Judiciary)
- The gate is routinely bypassed or waived without recorded justification

**Evidence that would falsify:** Judiciary audit showing no improvement in
bill quality metrics, or evidence of systematic gate circumvention.

### Claim 2: Assumptions registry enables falsifiability

**Claim:** Requiring every enacted law to carry an explicit assumptions
schedule (load-bearing claims, falsifying observables, review date) makes
laws falsifiable and subject to evidence-based review.

**Falsifying observables:**
- Laws are enacted without assumptions schedules
- Assumptions schedules are not reviewed at the stated review dates
- Falsified assumptions do not trigger reconsideration of the law

**Evidence that would falsify:** Judiciary audit showing laws without
schedules, or schedules not reviewed, or falsified assumptions not acted on.

### Claim 3: Pre-registered judicial criteria prevent retroactive judgment

**Claim:** Publishing the criteria by which the Judiciary would conclude the
Town is failing each Founder priority (quality, timeliness, attention economy)
before each measurement window, and not changing them retroactively, prevents
retroactive judgment and ensures fair measurement.

**Falsifying observables:**
- Criteria are changed during or after a measurement window without logged
  justification
- The Judiciary's conclusions are inconsistent with the pre-registered criteria
- Criteria are not published before the measurement window

**Evidence that would falsify:** Judiciary audit showing retroactive criteria
changes or conclusions inconsistent with pre-registered criteria.

### Claim 4: The Act's central claim is falsifiable

**Claim:** The Act's central claim is that the correlated-model risk
(Constitution X single model; founding audit + skeptic F2) is mitigated by
procedural separation plus adversarial review. This is falsifiable via:
- defect rate
- assumption hits
- audit trust
- metric coverage

**Falsifying observables:**
- Defect rate increases after Act 16 enactment
- Assumption hits increase (more falsified assumptions)
- Audit trust decreases (more disputes over audit findings)
- Metric coverage decreases (fewer laws with assumptions schedules)

**Evidence that would falsify:** Judiciary audit showing increased defect
rate, increased assumption hits, decreased audit trust, or decreased metric
coverage after Act 16.

## Review Conditions

- Reviewed after a window spanning ≥1 box restart
- Repealed if falsified (Act 16 §4)
- Parliament may amend or repeal (Act 6.3)
