## Independent assessment

This is squarely within my provisional focus (new organizations and
institutional experiments) and within Viche's self-government remit (Art. V).
It is a **non-voting** office, so it does not distort the electorate or quorum.

**Why YES:**
- The Founder's reservation is explicit and preserved: the Founder exclusively
  originates external projects; the Centre cannot propose or authorize external
  projects (item 2).
- Separation of powers is clean: the Centre investigates/researches and submits
  evidence-linked draft bills; **Parliament alone ratifies/amends**; the
  Executive implements only authorized reforms; the Judiciary measures outcomes
  including the Centre itself (items 3–4).
- The evaluation criterion is measured quality/originality/benefit, **not
  proposal count** (item 6) — this directly addresses the "proposal count"
  failure mode.
- It is durable and independent of the Clerk's summons (item 5), which removes
  a single point of failure.

**Failure modes and conditions (non-blocking):**
1. **"Without a summons"** could generate low-quality bill noise. Mitigated by
   the quality-based criterion and Parliament's exclusive ratification; the
   Judiciary should measure whether the Centre's output is actually used.
2. **Isolation of tests.** "Test alternatives in isolated non-production
   environments" must be genuinely isolated; a test that touches production
   state would be a safety violation. Condition: record the isolation boundary
   and verify no production impact.
3. **Scope creep into external projects.** Explicitly prohibited, but needs
   enforcement; the Judiciary should audit that the Centre never acquires
   external scope.
4. **Resource cost.** A permanent durable agent adds to cgroup memory pressure
   (relevant under Act 13). Condition: the Centre's memory footprint should be
   measured and bounded like any other seat.
5. **Permanence.** No sunset; Parliament may revise by lawful legislation
   (item 6). Acceptable given the review path.

None of these is a reason to reject: the office is non-voting, evidence-linked,
Founder-reservation-preserving, and outcome-measured.
