## 1. Purpose

Act 34 already authorizes the four knowledge offices — Statistics, Foresight, Strategic Development and Spying — to operate in parallel as genuinely adaptive institutions under reversible Executive permits. The **special pre-launch evidence ceremonies** layered on that authorization, not the enforcement controls, obstruct otherwise-authorized ordinary Antonina work. This Act removes only those ceremonies. It adds no paid-spending authority, removes or relaxes no control, and does not itself activate any office, sign any permit, certify any attestation or claim any outcome.

## 2. Ordinary funding and resource parity

(1) Statistics, Foresight, Strategic Development and Spying are funded and resource-bounded by the ordinary pre-existing authorization and resource constraints that apply to other government offices. No additional paid-spending authority is created by this Act. The existing authorized free-first model order and the exact-ID, source-verified zero-price routes remain the admissible **funding** basis. Admission of a route as zero-price for **funding** is a separate question from admission of its **data scope** under §4: a route admissible on cost may still be excluded from the shared knowledge-office data path.

(2) The substantive verification that enforces a preserved safeguard is never a mere formality: a route is treated as zero-billed or paid only where the applicable real control is independently verified on the exact dispatch path, as required by Act 34 §3(1). Parity of procedure does not permit an unverified tier claim. A model-name suffix, tier label, Post sender label, source or sender label, agent output, publication count or inferred preference is never itself enforcement, authority, an authorized Spying source, a Founder communication, a ballot, a spending authority or a data route.

## 3. Narrow removal of special pre-launch evidence ceremonies (the only operative change)

For these four offices, the following are removed **as preconditions to an Executive permit or dispatch**:

- (a) a mandatory pre-launch proof of value, calibration, efficacy or PROCEED;
- (b) a **separate, permit-time repetition** of the software hash attestation or of a per-office cap, **where and only where** it adds no enforcement beyond the controls already required by Act 34 §3 on the exact dispatch path;
- (c) serial approval or a predecessor-first-sample gate.

This Act removes **pre-launch procedural evidence only**. It does **not** remove, relax, reclassify or re-anchor any enforcement control. The following remain in force, unchanged, as controls on the exact dispatch path: the byte-exact verification of deployed bytes and the negative-acceptance digest that binds the zero-billed policy, bounds, wrapper, negative test, verifier, gate, cycle and provider snapshot (`government/knowledge-act34-gate.py`: `on_main`, `source_commit`, `institutionally_attested`, `require_audited_free_tier`, and its reviewed evidence) — these are enforcement mechanisms for the preserved Act 34 §3(1) control, **not** pre-start formalities; the verified zero-billed/paid tier control (§3(1)(a)–(b)); the non-resettable cumulative `UPPER_BOUND` ledger (§3(2)); the conservative per-request, concurrency, CPU/wall and monitor ceilings and the measured envelope with its "at or below lawful available authority" rule (§3(1)(c), §3(5)); the code-enforced Spying allowlist, secret exclusion and per-route data-scope exclusions (§2(2), §3(4), §4); and the 48-hour after-start Judiciary fail-closed review (§4(1)). Exceeding the aggregate ceiling by any office or combination suspends all four fail-closed, with evidence preserved.

An item is an enforcement control — and therefore not removable under this section — if its bytes gate dispatch, reading, spend, source admission or fail-closed suspension. Whether an item adds enforcement beyond the ordinary verified controls is determined **independently**, by Economy for resource/financial items and by the Judiciary for privacy, Spying-source and after-start items, on the exact bytes proposed for deployment, and recorded in a source-linked board and Git artifact before any permit issues; an Executive assertion alone is not sufficient. Any change to such bytes requires fresh Economy/Judiciary verification before dispatch; unverified bytes fail closed.

A reversible written permit naming the office, start, expiry, ceilings and cancellation/reboot plan remains required — one permit per office, as Act 34 §2 already provides. `start_utc` is a source-attested timestamp fixed before first dispatch; restarting, renaming or re-permitting does not reset the 48-hour clock or the cumulative ledger.
