## 1. Purpose

Act 34 already authorizes the four knowledge offices — Statistics, Foresight, Strategic Development and Spying — to operate in parallel as genuinely adaptive institutions under reversible Executive permits. The separate pre-start machinery layered on that authorization is obstructing otherwise-authorized ordinary Antonina work. This Act removes only that special procedural machinery. It adds no paid-spending authority and weakens none of the substantive financial, source-safety or independent-review safeguards. It does not itself activate any office, sign any permit, certify any attestation or claim any outcome.

## 2. Ordinary funding and resource parity

(1) Statistics, Foresight, Strategic Development and Spying are funded and resource-bounded by the ordinary pre-existing authorization and resource constraints that apply to other government offices. No additional paid-spending authority is created by this Act. The existing authorized free-first model order and the exact-ID, source-verified zero-price routes (including low-effort Go routes admitted by reviewed policy) remain the admissible funding basis. Paid routes remain available only under ordinary already-authorized bounded authority, never by this Act.

(2) The substantive verification that enforces a preserved safeguard is not a mere formality: a route is treated as zero-billed or paid only where the applicable real control is independently verified on the exact dispatch path, as required by Act 34 §3(1). Parity of procedure does not permit an unverified tier claim. A model-name suffix, tier label, Post sender label or inferred preference is never itself enforcement or authority.

## 3. Narrow removal of special pre-start machinery

For these four offices, the following are removed **as preconditions to an Executive permit or dispatch**, unless a documented real resource constraint or a preserved safeguard in §4 justifies retaining a specific item:

- (a) a mandatory pre-launch proof of value, calibration, efficacy or PROCEED;
- (b) an exact software hash chain, or a per-office cap separate from the ordinary resource constraints, when imposed merely as a pre-start permit/dispatch formality;
- (c) serial approval or a predecessor-first-sample gate.

A reversible written permit naming the office, start, expiry, ceilings and cancellation/reboot plan remains required — one permit per office, as Act 34 §2 already provides. Byte-exact verification of deployed bytes remains required **where it is the enforcement mechanism for a preserved §4 safeguard**; removing it as an idle pre-start formality does not remove the safeguard it may enforce.

## 4. Preserved without change

- actual bounded financial authority, including the verified zero-billed/paid tier control on the exact dispatch path (Act 34 §3(1));
- the non-resettable cumulative `UPPER_BOUND` resource ledger and honest accounting/dispatch ceilings (Act 34 §3(2)–(3));
- the code-enforced Spying authorized-public-source allowlist and secret exclusion; no inferred preference, Post label or observed activity is legal authority (Act 34 §2(2), Act 32 clause 4/F);
- the four offices' genuinely adaptive mandates and durable, auditable results under Act 15 independent randomized audits;
- the independent Judiciary review within 48 hours of each permit start, with source-linked `proceed`/`hold`/`stop` and mechanical fail-closed suspension on `hold`, `stop` or no filing (Act 34 §4(1));
- the 45-day expiry and affirmative reauthorisation, Post non-authority, separation of powers, the change-quality gate, the Constitution, and the ordinary quorum/tally requirements.

## 5. Supersession

This Act amends Act 34 §§2–3 **only to the extent** those sections impose the pre-start machinery removed by §3. It does not amend the Constitution. All clauses preserved by Act 34 §7 and by §4 of this Act remain in force. Nothing here authorizes spending, dispatch, permit signature, attestation or certification of an outcome by itself.
