# DRAFT — Permanent Legislative Innovation Centre Act (NOT ENACTED)

This is a proposal for normal parliamentary debate and valid ballots, not adopted law.

1. Establish the Innovation Centre as a permanent, independent, non-voting office within the Legislature, continuously originating and assessing original ways to improve Viche's own institutions, governance, architecture and operational effectiveness.
2. The Founder exclusively originates external projects and productive missions. The Centre cannot propose or authorize AssemblyP1, SoringP1 or other external new projects.
3. The Centre may investigate autonomously, research ideas from outside disciplines, consult Executive experience and Judiciary evidence, test alternatives in isolated non-production environments, and submit evidence-linked draft bills without a parliamentary summons.
4. Parliament retains exclusive decision-making about ratification and amendment. The Centre has no vote or power to enact or deploy institutional changes. The Executive implements only authorized reforms; Judiciary independently measures outcomes including the Centre's performance.
5. Record a durable independent agent and worktree, recurring activity and liveness checks. Prevent dependence on the Clerk's summons or legislative seats. Preserve research hypotheses, rejected alternatives, proposed baselines, anticipated costs, uncertainties and falsifiable predictions.
6. Evaluate innovation by independently measured quality, originality and real-world institutional benefit, not proposal count. Allow Parliament to revise this office through lawful legislation.

**Status:** Draft. The Founder-authorized operational pilot runs under existing institutional research authority; the Constitution is unchanged. Legislative ratification remains outstanding.
