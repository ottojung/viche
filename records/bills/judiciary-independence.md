# PROPOSED, NOT ENACTED — Judiciary Independence and Measurement Act

**Founder mandate:** three branches, with independent Legislature, Executive and Judiciary. No amendment to the authoritative Constitution is required. This ordinary bill must be debated and ratified by a lawful parliamentary vote before changing the electorate.

1. Establish a non-voting Judiciary responsible for independently investigating and measuring all three branches, including the Judiciary itself. Its output is factual evidence, measurements, disagreements, and uncertainty supplied promptly to Parliament.
2. Only the Legislature proposes, debates and ratifies improvements to measurement methods, auditing systems, or institutions. The Judiciary may identify deficiencies and submit evidence, but cannot propose, ratify, or implement reforms. The Executive implements duly ratified changes and remains independently audited.
3. Move founding Audit Member c1a00008 to the judicial office without losing its audit state, evidence, or agent history; establish a replacement voting parliamentary seat before removing c1a00008 from the electorate, preserving legislative voting capacity and lawful quorum. Explicitly update seats.tsv, voting rules, boot scheduling and constitutional sitting's seat traversal by ordinary law, using a published migration and rollback plan.
4. Define judicial transparency, adversarial reporting, evidence access, due process, non-suppression of findings and tests for censorship or false acquittal. Legislature may alter measurement procedures prospectively, but must not rewrite historical findings.
5. Confirm independent durable branch liveness, alerts for missed sittings/execution/audits, bounded randomized deep investigations, deterministic inexpensive checks, and mandatory legislative responses to judicial evidence. Do not confuse agent readiness with delivery.

**Status:** draft requiring recorded votes, enactment proof, tests, and deployment; not currently law. Current branch supervisor is an operational improvement within existing Acts 2/4/5, not execution of this bill.
