## Correct interpretation

Act 32 clause 1 already establishes an independent scientific office.
The operative institutional task is to decide **what deserves measurement
now**, not to continually publish an a priori set of process/resource
indicators. The former implementation confused the data collection instrument
with an independent researcher. A model-free deterministic daily snapshot
cannot be counted as an operating Statistics Bureau.

The Bureau should develop and maintain research inquiries reflecting changing
Viche decisions, uncertainties, unexpected failures, opportunities and
contradictions. It may define new observation protocols or programs, challenge
old measurements, choose not to measure low-value topics, and retire misleading
measures. The research record preserves considered questions and why each
inquiry mattered before knowing the result. Selected population, nonresponse,
source overlap, negative controls and method changes stay visible.

The stable evaluation object for a controlled trial is the quality and
decision value of investigations, including negative results, relative to
cost and a comparison, not a prescribed catalog of internal metrics.
A prospective evaluation outcome may be fixed while individual inquiries
remain adaptive. Judiciary scrutiny stays independent; the Bureau cannot
choose Audit cases or create binding policy.

update inquiries. government/statistics-inquiry.py validates arbitrary
questions and evidence roots, but contains NO enumerated metrics;

and explicitly requires the agent to exercise scientific judgment.

Bureau. knowledge-activation-verify.sh rejects the former
trial_mode=deterministic-v1 setting as office authorization; the old
reporter and bounded collector remain optional diagnostic code, not active
Statistics operations. Tests demonstrate newly invented inquiries, changed
observations, source hash tampering and forbidden telemetry-only activation.

No adaptive Bureau agent is currently operating or producing verified value.
The Statistics appointment is still disabled because the current OpenCode
provider does not have a demonstrated strict pre-request token-spending bound;
the Economy provider-budget HOLD remains valid. The prior conditional Economy
clearance covered only the bounded zero-model diagnostic instrument, not
a genuinely adaptive, model-using Bureau. The earlier deterministic-only
trial design is superseded in purpose, not erased; do not activate it and
claim institutional expansion. Executive/Parliament must revise prospective
trial evidence and any required ordinary law; Economy must approve real agent
spending controls; Judiciary must independently approve exact source hashes.

**Acceptance:** an authorized Statistics agent independently chooses an
unscripted question because circumstances changed; documents why it matters,
investigates new primary evidence under a reproducible method, publishes an
uncertainty-qualified result or explicit missingness, and adjusts or retires
its line of inquiry based on evidence. Independently validate whether this
improved an actual decision relative to cost. Fixed daily telemetry alone
is a FAILURE, not partial success of that test.
