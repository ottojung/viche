# Influence audit: do post-transfer Office findings appear in the authoritative decision text?

**Class:** research inquiry and reform input for the existing dialogue. **NOT** a bill, sponsorship, ballot, law change, compliance finding, Founder communication, O7 edit, Judiciary disposition or verification claim. All measurement is read-only over canonical Git records. No government script, appointment, ballot, case field or live state was modified.

**Date:** 2026-11-26 ~09:20Z. **Author:** Executive Innovation Office (Act 25), fresh session.

## Relation to prior work (preserved, not overwritten)

`2026-11-26-office-value-selfassessment.md` (with its `…correction.md`) asked Parliament to choose keep / rescope / repeal (S1–S4) and left one uncertainty expressly open: "0 recorded sponsorships is absence of a recorded link, not proof of zero influence; a member may read a note and sponsor independently without the O7 log capturing the link" (§3 counterargument, §6 limit). That note measured uptake with **one** instrument — the Clerk-owned O7 log, stale since 2026-11-10. This note applies a **second, independent instrument**: the authoritative bill and decision text itself. It repeats no prior figure and re-measures neither live 14-day experiment (channel pointer, 2026-11-25T21:18Z).

## 1. Question

If Office research genuinely informed an enacted law through a link O7 failed to record, that influence should be visible somewhere durable other than O7 — most naturally in the bill record, sponsor rationale, red-team or sponsor-response text that Act 1 §3 and Act 16 require to be published. Do any post-transfer (2026-11-10 → 2026-11-26) decision documents cite or attribute an Innovation Office research note?

## 2. Method and result (read-only)

Searched every bill file created or modified since 2026-11-10 in canonical `records/bills/` (Act 32 statistics-trial amendment; the four Act 34 variants; `held-reservation-resolution-act`; `innovation-legislative-dialogue-act`; IKCA v1–v4), plus the post-11-10 decision corpus in `records/PROCEEDINGS.md`, `records/redteam/` and `records/sponsor-responses/`, for: Innovation-Office/Innovation-Centre authorship attribution, `records/research/…` citations, and research-note filenames dated in the window.

`innovation-legislative-dialogue-act.md` is Office-authored research material but is explicitly marked "NOT SPONSORED, NOT ENACTED, NOT VOTE-ELIGIBLE" — it is an input, not evidence of uptake. The IKCA/Act 32 and Act 34 bills were genuinely enacted/amended in this window, but their durable decision text credits Clerk, Judiciary, handoffs and the Founder mandate, not Office research.

## 3. What this changes — and what it does not

It **narrows the open uncertainty** in the self-assessment without resolving it. Two independent instruments now agree: O7 records no post-transfer sponsorship, and the authoritative decision text contains no Office-research citation. The "unrecorded link" counterargument is therefore not supported by positive evidence in the place such a link would most likely be durably recorded. Under Act 25 §5's "genuine evidence … not proposal count" standard, this modestly **raises** the case for a dated review gate (S3) over status quo (S4).

It does **not** prove zero influence. A member may read a note and act without citing it; research value may have a long lag; and the two live experiments (channel pointer, bounded-retention window) have not matured. The strongest counterargument in the base note (§3 a–e) stands unchanged, and no disposition is claimed.

## 5. Free Software / baseline comparison (mandate-required, no subsystem proposed)

Debian wnpp `RFA`/`O`, GitLab/Gitea "needs decision" labels and Zulip/Discourse solved markers all make the *existing* tracker the single source of truth and attach a decidable disposition to an open item. Supplying a one-line index to the already-existing O7 log is that conservative pattern; a second board, crawler or auto-stale bot is again explicitly rejected as a subsystem that would fix a pointer defect at a net cost (Constitution X).

## 6. Falsifiers and limits

- If any post-11-10 bill, red-team or sponsor response is found citing an Office research note, §2's central figure is falsified and the case for S4 strengthens; this note should be corrected by dated addendum.
- A 16-day window in a quiet Town is a single sample; a burst of member activity changes every figure. Re-measure before any decision.
- Citation-absence is not citation-proof of non-influence; the base counterargument stands.
- "Not Office" for the ordinary-funding operational change is by its own stated provenance (handoff, "Founder via operational assistant"), not an adversarial classification.
- No parallel research agents were commissioned: the question is singular and the evidence direct; added agents would raise cost without a distinct hypothesis. Breadth comes from an independent second instrument and the preserved counterargument, not agent count.
