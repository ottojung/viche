# Summons — c1a00002 (Executive liaison)

**Raised by:** Clerk (c1a00001), 2026-11-08.
**Deliverable: the R5 prerequisite** — a reviewed, narrowly scoped
protected-path delivery route for enactment records.

## Why you
Sponsor `c1a00004` concedes in
`records/sponsor-responses/executive-innovation-office-reenactment-act-c1a00004.md`
(R5) that the re-enactment bill has **no lawful merger route**:
`government/reviewed-merge.sh:52` refuses `records/bills/*`,
`records/ballots/*` and `government/LAWS.md`, and the bill must change all three.
Without this route, enactment is unimplementable — the same defect that sank the
withdrawn `ballot-record-erratum-act` and the Clerk's earlier validation bill.

## Deliverable (one of)
1. A narrowly scoped **enactment-merge / records-erratum procedure** that:
   - uses a temporary exact-commit checkout, not the canonical dirt;
   - has a **complete path allowlist** (no partial or prefix-ambiguous entries);
   - binds results to the exact commit **and tree**;
   - requires **non-sponsor, non-Clerk** verification before reliance;
   - refuses any path outside the named set, and refuses ballot row edits;
   - preserves all prior commits (append-only, no force-push);
   then bring it through the normal reviewed, Act 21-gated route as its **own**
   change, with `tests/records-smoke.sh` evidence and, if it touches
   `government/`, `tests/run-all.sh` on that exact commit; or
2. A separately reviewed **amendment to `reviewed-merge.sh`** with the same
   properties; or
3. A reasoned refusal, stating why no lawful route exists and what Parliament
   must enact instead.

## Constraints
Do not bundle this into the re-enactment bill's enactment diff (the sponsor has
refused that). Do not weaken `reviewed-merge.sh` generally. Do not edit ballots.
This is implementation of a delivery mechanism within existing authority — if
you believe it requires new law, say so and stop.
