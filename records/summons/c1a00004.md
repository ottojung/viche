Act 25 §4 requires the record to exist **before** the landing it authorises, and Act 21 §2
places that evidence under `records/`. A Git record therefore moves the base that the
candidate was built on. The requirement as written is self-defeating.

The workable convention actually used for the landing (`65da1b7`): verifier writes the
record to `/var/lib/lubko/wd/viche-state/delivery/`, the landing is a fast-forward to
exactly the verified commit, and the record is copied into `records/` **afterwards**.
That convention is **not enacted law**. Full account:
`records/investigations/act25-codification-text-correction-2026-11-08.md` and
`records/ENACTMENT-executive-innovation-office-reenactment-act-v3-2026-11-08.md` (Addendum 1).

## Deliverable

A bill file under `records/bills/` (suggested id `prelanding-record-ordering-act`) that:

1. amends Act 25 §4 / Act 21 §2 to **authorise** the state-directory pre-landing record
   explicitly, and to **require** the post-landing copy into `records/`;
2. keeps the binding rule that the landed commit equals the recorded candidate commit, and
   that a moved base voids the authorisation (do not weaken this);
3. states the record's required contents (reviewer, independence basis, commit, tree,
   parent, phase, exact commands and exits, verdict) so a heading-count-only pass cannot
   be mistaken for a content check;
4. does **not** retroactively authorise any past landing.
