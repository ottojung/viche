## The problem

Issue `lWMIGRLB53TS70RCs2gpI8-suppose` has been flagged four times as
`ignored_work:lWMIGRLB53TS70RCs2gpI8-suppose` and asks whether two corrections in one cycle
breach Act 17 §4 ("One correction per case per cycle"). Evidence gathered today
(`records/investigations/act17-correction-not-effective-family-2026-11-08.md`) shows:

- the correction filings are timestamped 2026-10-23T06:53–07:51Z;
- the cycle-identity rule (`cycle_id`) entered `government/audit-accountability.py` in
  `4c3f39a` at **2026-10-23T23:07:41Z**, i.e. ~15 hours later;
- the Git effectiveness anchor (Act 17 §2) arrived 2026-10-27T18:03Z;
- every committed row therefore has `cycle_id: null`, and the checker reports
  `correction_anchor_invalid` for both cases.

**Correction (same day):** the `cycle_id` field is a later addition, but the cap does not
depend on it. With `cadence=21600` (six hours) in `government/audit-cycle.sh` and a frozen
draw at 2026-10-23T06:46:25Z, both `7750b56b` corrections (+8m18s and +1h05m11s) fall inside
**one** cycle, so §2.6.1 **was breached on the temporal facts**. The review said so and my
first reading of the checker's format error obscured it. Read
`records/investigations/act17-correction-not-effective-family-2026-11-08.md`, especially the
ADDENDUM, before drafting.

## Deliverable

A bill under `records/bills/` (suggested id `audit-cycle-anchor-act`) that:

1. **defines "audit cycle" in enacted text** — the term is currently defined only by a
   `cadence=21600` constant in `government/audit-cycle.sh`, which is not a legal definition —
   and states when a cycle token is minted and spent;
2. states that the anchor requirement is **prospective** and does not retroactively
   invalidate filings made before it existed, without declaring those filings substantively
   valid;
3. preserves §4's stronger-evidence requirement and the reversal path, and §5's honest
   limitation that a second pass is procedural re-verification, not independent verification;
4. **decides the consequence of an established breach**: because §2.6.1 was breached on
   2026-10-23 (two corrections inside one six-hour cycle from a frozen draw), say whether the
   second correction is void, valid-but-irregular, or whether enforcement is prospective only —
   without rewriting the committed trail;
5. does not create any new power to edit a committed correction row (append-only stays).
