## Deliverable

A bill under `records/bills/` (suggested id `audit-cycle-anchor-act`) that:

1. **defines "audit cycle" in enacted text** — the term is currently defined only by a
   `cadence=21600` constant in `government/audit-cycle.sh`, which is not a legal definition —
   and states when a cycle token is minted and spent;
2. states that the anchor requirement is **prospective** and does not retroactively
   invalidate filings made before it existed, without declaring those filings substantively
   valid;

   limitation that a second pass is procedural re-verification, not independent verification;

   2026-10-23 (two corrections inside one six-hour cycle from a frozen draw), say whether the
   second correction is void, valid-but-irregular, or whether enforcement is prospective only —
   without rewriting the committed trail;
5. does not create any new power to edit a committed correction row (append-only stays).
