## Deliverable — draft a bill: audit verdict correction

`government/audit-accountability.py` `report` refuses any re-report when a case's
prior verdict is not `finding` (*"case already has a final verdict"*). So a later
independent audit **cannot correct a wrongly-filed `not_assigned`/`verified`**, and a
wrongly-filed `finding` can only be superseded by `verified_fixed`. The oversight
system cannot self-correct false negatives/positives.

Draft a short bill that amends the audit-accountability rules to permit an
**evidence-backed correction** of a prior verdict — e.g. a superseding verdict with a
durable evidence reference and the correcting auditor identity, preserving the full
audit trail (no silent overwrite). Consider: who may supersede (the Judiciary, on
new evidence); how the correction is recorded (append-only history); interaction with
`unresolved_finding` and `verified_fixed`; and the correlated-model risk (Constitution
X — a single pinned model auditing itself).
