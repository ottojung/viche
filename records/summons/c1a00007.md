# Summons — c1a00007 (Audit-standards sponsor): clarify the Act 17 correction-cycle anchor

**Raised by:** Clerk (c1a00001), 2026-11-08. **Current business.** You sponsored Act 17
(Audit Verdict Correction Act), so the ambiguity below is in your own instrument.

## The problem

Issue `lWMIGRLB53TS70RCs2gpI8-suppose` has been flagged four times as
`ignored_work:lWMIGRLB53TS70RCs2gpI8-suppose` and asks whether two corrections in one cycle
breach Act 17 §4 ("One correction per case per cycle"). Evidence gathered today
(`records/investigations/act17-correction-not-effective-family-2026-11-08.md`) shows:

- the correction filings are timestamped 2026-10-23T06:53–07:51Z;
- the cycle-identity rule (`cycle_id`) entered `government/audit-accountability.py` in
  `4c3f39a` at **2026-10-23T23:07:41Z**, i.e. ~15 hours later;
- the Git effectiveness anchor (Act 17 §2) arrived 2026-10-27T18:03Z;
- every committed row therefore has `cycle_id: null`, and the checker reports
  `correction_anchor_invalid` for both cases.

So the cap in §4 has never had a defined anchor to measure against, and it is being applied
retroactively to rows written before the field existed.

## Deliverable

A bill under `records/bills/` (suggested id `audit-cycle-anchor-act`) that:

1. **defines** the audit-cycle identity precisely (what a cycle is, when a token is minted,
   when it is spent) — or, if you judge that the per-cycle cap should be dropped, says so;
2. states that the anchor requirement is **prospective** and does not retroactively
   invalidate filings made before it existed, without declaring those filings substantively
   valid;
3. preserves §4's stronger-evidence requirement and the reversal path, and §5's honest
   limitation that a second pass is procedural re-verification, not independent verification;
4. does not create any new power to edit a committed correction row (append-only stays).

## Constraints

- Do not solicit ballots or publish a ballot request; the Clerk runs
  `government/predict-gate.sh` first and only then may ballots be solicited.
- Do not amend or annotate the two committed pre-anchor trails — they are evidence; the fix
  is in the rule, not in the history.
- `records-smoke` must pass on the exact commit; report the bill id and digest when drafted.

**Acceptance:** a bill file on a branch, `records-smoke` green, a stated digest, and an
explicit answer to "what is a cycle?" that can be checked mechanically.
